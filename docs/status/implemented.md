## Implemented behavior in source
- **Steam Boiler Array:** a separate Horizon controller recognizes shells with 3–7 blocks on each footprint axis and 3–16 blocks high, aligned behind the controller's facing. Corners must be casing; for footprints larger than 3×3 the other shell edges must also be casing; remaining shell faces allow casing or unformed Pressure Glass; the interior must be air. Heat capacity and maximum heat rate scale by source sections. Auto/Steam/High-Pressure/Superheated modes, temperature bands, heat costs/savings, steam lifting/downgrading, pressure venting, over-temperature venting, and status smoothing are implemented in the boiler simulation. Water buckets feed slot 0 and empty buckets return in slot 1; a Wrench reports status and cycles pressure, or sets facing when used on another horizontal side. Each fluid tank remains fixed at 48,000 mB because Horizon's current fluid-capacity hook cannot select capacity from the target block entity. The separate controller and its placement remain Horizon alpha scaffolding. The expanded behavior compiles remotely but has not been verified in a Horizon client or in-game.
- **Steam Turbine Array:** the Rust alpha forms Arcforge-sized 3–7 cross-section by 3–27 length shells, accepts the three steam grades and lubricant families, produces Exhaust Steam and FE with spin-up, lubrication and vacuum bonuses, and exposes Wrench status. Horizon capabilities are fixed by block ID, so its controller has fixed tank sizes and all-face ports; runtime behavior is still unverified.
- **Condenser Array:** a separate controller forms 3–7 block boxes, accepts Exhaust Steam and condenses it into water 1:1. The source open-air rate is 400 mB/t per 27 blocks, with touching water/ice bonuses and an 800 mB/t per-cube cap. Fixed 203,259 mB tanks represent the maximum source-sized tank; climate multipliers and size-scaled capacity are simplified. The Wrench reports formation, size, rate, and both tanks. No in-game behavior is verified.

- **Pressurized Conduits:** four tiers route the 22 Arcforge gases listed in the pinned source through separate networks at 400/1,600/6,400/25,600 mB/t, with recipes, tags, and loot. The gas list matches upstream `gas_properties.json`. This source compiles and the content archive passes Horizon's checker; arbitrary third-party gas classification is unsupported by the pinned API, and network behavior is not in-world verified.
- **Pressurized Cylinders and Gas Cartridges:** four Cylinder blocks store 64,000 / 256,000 / 1,024,000 / 4,096,000 mB and only accept the 22 Arcforge gas IDs recognized by Pressurized Conduits. Four Gas Cartridge items hold 32,000 / 128,000 / 512,000 / 2,048,000 mB and transfer at 2,000 / 8,000 / 32,000 / 128,000 mB per tick. The Cylinder's top inventory slot drains a cartridge into its tank; the bottom slot fills a cartridge. A saved level property drives the source five-stage gauge. A disposable-world runtime smoke verified one Wrought cartridge draining 32,000 mB oxygen into a Wrought Cylinder, leaving the cartridge empty and updating the gauge to level 2. A new existing-hook interaction lets a player use a charged cartridge on a Cylinder to insert gas or an empty cartridge to extract gas at its tier rate; this new path compiles but has not yet been runtime-checked. A per-tick snapshot preserves the gas on ordinary block drops, and placement restores it. Four source-pattern recipes exist for each tier family; Horizon's crafting hook does not expose input components, so shaped tier-upgrade recipes do not preserve stored gas. Player-facing menu access, other tiers/gases, six-sided automation, gas tint/comparator, and reload persistence remain unverified or unfinished. Unknown third-party gas identifiers remain unsupported by the pinned API.

- **Heat Cells:** all four tiers are registered with source-derived HU capacities, transfer rates, maximum temperatures, and passive loss rates. Stored HU persists in block-entity data, transfers only downhill through adjacent supported heat connections within the tier's total per-tick rate, and updates the existing five-level heat blockstate. Mining and placement carry charge in custom data. Four source-shaped crafting recipes and explosion-aware loot tables are packaged. The alpha automatically connects all six sides; custom screens, capsule slots, insulation upgrades, redstone controls, side configuration, and charge-preserving tier upgrades are not implemented. No in-world transfer, loss, persistence, or visual check has been run.

- **Item Conduits:** all four source tiers are registered with their Arcforge models, source loot tables, and survival recipes. Connected runs are cached and invalidated on conduit placement, breaking, Wrench changes, or a changed local conduit edge. They use Horizon's transactional item-move helper between configured Output and Input faces. Each network uses its slowest tier: 8/16/32/64 items every 20/10/5/2 ticks. The Wrench changes a face through Auto, Input, Output, and Disabled; Output faces extract from adjacent inventories and Input faces insert into them. A refused transfer stays at its source. Animated packets, conduit-held items, filters, and capability-based Auto detection are absent. Tier upgrades use shaped recipes and do not preserve Wrench face settings. Routes and transfers have not been tested in-world.

Heat flows to supported adjacent consumers or thermal conduits at up to 100 HU per call, including through thermal conduits to the Thermoelectric Plant. The source-shaped recipe, block registration, and loot table are included. Heat Upgrades increase lava and passive-contact heat output by 12.5% each, up to eight (+100%); the count is saved on the plant. Redstone control, side configuration, and a dedicated screen remain absent; the supported-neighbor transfer is an alpha simplification.
- **Fuel Burner/Fluid Tanks:** Four tank tiers store 16,000 / 64,000 / 256,000 / 1,024,000 mB. All 16 pinned fuel profiles are recognized. - **Energy Conduits and Wrench:** Wrought, Tempered, Hardened, and Arcforged tiers provide source rates of 256, 1,024, 8,192, and 262,144 FE/t. Bidirectionally connected members share the lowest tier's rate and a member-scaled pool; one-way Output-to-Input links transfer between separate pools. Mod-side producer input and network output each have a shared per-network rate budget, and pools distribute FE to supported Arcforge machines within their insertion limits and capacities. The scheduler uses the current block entity passed to Horizon's tick hook because that entity is absent from ordinary world lookups during its tick. Horizon's native energy capability remains per block and all-sided, so external calls bypass conduit face modes and the shared rate; the mod reconciles those changes before pooled writes, though direct calls may temporarily raise capacity. Previously saved FE from the old 50,000 FE per-block buffers is preserved; excess charge drains and the pool capacity returns to its normal member-rate limit. The Wrench cycles each face independently between Auto, Input, Output, and Disabled, including when the neighboring block is another conduit; the visible connection and pool topology respect configured direction. Rotation currently changes facing state only; sided inventory/energy behavior remains all-side. The Wrought recipe, Wrench recipe, and all four conduit loot tables are source-identical.
