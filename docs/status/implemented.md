# Current implementation

This is an as-built snapshot, not a claim of Arcforge parity. **Current slice:** four portable FE Battery tiers use Horizon's native item-energy capability at Arcforge capacities and rates, with four source-pattern recipes. Pressurized Cylinders and Gas Cartridges store and transfer the 22 pinned Arcforge gas IDs through existing Horizon hooks. A disposable world verified Wrought cartridge insertion/extraction and gas persistence after save/reload. Commit `1e5d989` enlarges the five-stage gauge, uses a cutout render layer for the Cylinder's alpha texture, and adds a Wrench action-bar readout; the user confirmed the matching client in-game. Charged/empty Energy items transfer FE to/from Energy Cells using a hand interaction. Energy Cell enum states select the five fill models and sync from stored FE; their block items are single-stack to prevent stored-energy duplication. Wrench inspection reports exact FE and gauge level. GitHub run [43510577279](https://github.com/Sipper1236/arcforge-horizon/actions/runs/43510577279) passed both jobs and its client artifact checksums. These Energy Cell behavior changes still need in-game verification. Local Rust `cargo check` and Horizon's 400-recipe content build/check pass. The active completion target is in [goal.md](../goal.md), and the resumable project state is in [handoff.md](../handoff.md). For verification strength, see [verification.md](verification.md). The code targets Arcforge commit `8843cb02cf9132f278f6a8e804b6d78837d3cbcb` and Horizon Nightly 2026-11-08, commit `fdc87aecdbd116bf1e08adcd062fcbaea8c2e2ca`.

## Portable FE Batteries

Four battery items expose Horizon's native item-energy capability with source capacities/rates of 250,000 FE / 1,000 FE/t, 1,000,000 FE / 4,000 FE/t, 4,000,000 FE / 16,000 FE/t, and 16,000,000 FE / 64,000 FE/t. Four source-pattern crafting recipes and localized names are packaged. Shaped tier-upgrade crafting cannot preserve charge because the pinned crafting hook does not expose ingredient components. A mod-side hand interaction now transfers FE between a charged Arcforge energy item and an Energy Cell, or extracts FE into an empty item. It respects both item and cell rates/capacities, updates saved item/block data together, and reports amounts in the action bar. Transfers require a single item in the held stack. This is an alpha adaptation of Arcforge's two-slot GUI; the hand path still needs matching-client and save/reload verification. Lithium Cells and Battery Arrays are not implemented.

## Implemented behavior in source

- **Pressurized Conduits:** four tiers route the 22 Arcforge gases listed in the pinned source through separate networks at 400/1,600/6,400/25,600 mB/t, with recipes, tags, and loot. The gas list matches upstream `gas_properties.json`. This source compiles and the content archive passes Horizon's checker; arbitrary third-party gas classification is unsupported by the pinned API, and network behavior is not in-world verified.
- **Pressurized Cylinders and Gas Cartridges:** four Cylinder blocks store 64,000 / 256,000 / 1,024,000 / 4,096,000 mB and only accept the 22 Arcforge gas IDs recognized by Pressurized Conduits. Four Gas Cartridge items hold 32,000 / 128,000 / 512,000 / 2,048,000 mB and transfer at 2,000 / 8,000 / 32,000 / 128,000 mB per tick. The Cylinder's top inventory slot drains a cartridge into its tank; the bottom slot fills a cartridge. A saved level property drives the source five-stage gauge. A disposable-world runtime smoke verified one Wrought cartridge draining 32,000 mB oxygen into a Wrought Cylinder, leaving the cartridge empty and updating the gauge to level 2. A new existing-hook interaction lets a player use a charged cartridge on a Cylinder to insert gas or an empty cartridge to extract gas at its tier rate; this new path compiles but has not yet been runtime-checked. A per-tick snapshot preserves the gas on ordinary block drops, and placement restores it. Four source-pattern recipes exist for each tier family; Horizon's crafting hook does not expose input components, so shaped tier-upgrade recipes do not preserve stored gas. Player-facing menu access, other tiers/gases, six-sided automation, gas tint/comparator, and reload persistence remain unverified or unfinished. Unknown third-party gas identifiers remain unsupported by the pinned API.

- **Heat Cells:** all four tiers are registered with source-derived HU capacities, transfer rates, maximum temperatures, and passive loss rates. Stored HU persists in block-entity data, transfers only downhill through adjacent supported heat connections within the tier's total per-tick rate, and updates the existing five-level heat blockstate. Mining and placement carry charge in custom data. Four source-shaped crafting recipes and explosion-aware loot tables are packaged. The alpha automatically connects all six sides; custom screens, capsule slots, insulation upgrades, redstone controls, side configuration, and charge-preserving tier upgrades are not implemented. No in-world transfer, loss, persistence, or visual check has been run.

- **Item Conduits:** all four source tiers are registered with their Arcforge models, source loot tables, and survival recipes. Connected runs are cached and invalidated on conduit placement, breaking, Wrench changes, or a changed local conduit edge. They use Horizon's transactional item-move helper between configured Output and Input faces. Each network uses its slowest tier: 8/16/32/64 items every 20/10/5/2 ticks. The Wrench changes a face through Auto, Input, Output, and Disabled; Output faces extract from adjacent inventories and Input faces insert into them. A refused transfer stays at its source. Animated packets, conduit-held items, filters, and capability-based Auto detection are absent. Tier upgrades use shaped recipes and do not preserve Wrench face settings. Routes and transfers have not been tested in-world.

- **Metal Press:** upstream recipe selection, die matching, input counts, duration, outputs, and 20 FE/t. Dies are reusable and saved in inventory.
- **Sifter:** four upstream recipes, independent output rolls, mesh tiers, six output slots, and a 20,000 FE buffer.
- **Arc Crusher:** 83 recipe definitions, recipe timing/results/bonus chance/ore-yield handling, and output backpressure. A dedicated native screen shows input, main output, bonus output, FE/capacity, recipe progress, and inventory. The matching client completed one coal recipe, producing carbon dust after 200 ticks and consuming 4,000 FE; output backpressure and the remaining catalog need verification. Wrench inspection reports FE and processing progress for the Arc Crusher, Induction Furnace, Metal Press, and Sifter. The Crushing Array runs three lanes in a formed 3×3×3 casing cube, with the center casing as controller. Lane priority rotates each tick to prevent starvation when available FE cannot advance all active lanes. Placement and recovery ticks form complete cubes; breaking a casing unforms the remaining parts. Casing FE buffers consolidate into the center before processing. The source Speed and Energy Upgrades apply to the array. The fixed north-facing array now exposes source-default automation faces: top input slots 0–2, bottom output slots 3–8, and south/back FE input. Item insertion remains center-only. Using the Wrench on the center reports current FE and all three lane progress values in the action bar. The pinned API's block-wide capability hook cannot read the selected casing's saved side modes, so Wrench-configurable ports and facing selection remain absent; the older standalone controller id remains available for save compatibility.
- **Machine block drops:** source loot tables are now present for the Arc Crusher, Carbonizer, Fiberizer, Firebox, Induction Furnace, Infuser, Metal Press, Sifter, Steel Block, and Vulcanizer. Rates are 200 / 800 / 3,200 / 12,800 mB/t, limited by the slowest connected tier. - **Thermal Conduits:** all four source tiers are registered at 50, 200, 800, and 51,200 HU/t. The mod discovers connected groups, pools HU across bidirectionally connected members, uses the lowest member tier as the pool rate, and scales storage by member count. Source input and consumer output each use a shared tier-rate budget per tick. One-way Output-to-Input links transfer through the destination pool, preserving direction and applying each pool's budget. HU and hottest carried source temperature are saved across member block entities. The scheduler uses the current block entity passed to Horizon's tick hook because that entity is absent from ordinary world lookups during its tick. Firebox, Firebox Array, Geothermal, Fuel Burner, and Solar can feed the networks, which push into supported Fiberizer, Vulcanizer, Grain Dryer, and Thermoelectric consumers. The Wrench configures each face through Auto/Input/Output/Disabled. Source-shaped recipes preserve tier ingredients, patterns, and output counts; base-tier tags and block drops are included. Horizon has no heat capability hook, so transport runs in mod-side ticks. Color data/dyed variants, component-preserving upgrades, and in-world behavior remain incomplete or unverified.
- **Energy Conduits and Wrench:** Wrought, Tempered, Hardened, and Arcforged tiers provide source rates of 256, 1,024, 8,192, and 262,144 FE/t. Bidirectionally connected members share the lowest tier's rate and a member-scaled pool; one-way Output-to-Input links transfer between separate pools. Mod-side producer input and network output each have a shared per-network rate budget, and pools distribute FE to supported Arcforge machines within their insertion limits and capacities. The scheduler uses the current block entity passed to Horizon's tick hook because that entity is absent from ordinary world lookups during its tick. Horizon's native energy capability remains per block and all-sided, so external calls bypass conduit face modes and the shared rate; the mod reconciles those changes before pooled writes, though direct calls may temporarily raise capacity. Previously saved FE from the old 50,000 FE per-block buffers is preserved; excess charge drains and the pool capacity returns to its normal member-rate limit. The Wrench cycles each face independently between Auto, Input, Output, and Disabled, including when the neighboring block is another conduit; the visible connection and pool topology respect configured direction. It also configures energy output faces on the Combustion and Thermoelectric Plants and rotates horizontal facing clockwise on supported single-block machines; multiblock controllers and the Resin Tap are excluded. Rotation currently changes facing state only; sided inventory/energy behavior remains all-side. The Wrought recipe, Wrench recipe, and all four conduit loot tables are source-identical. Three tier-upgrade recipes retain source patterns and counts in vanilla shaped form; custom component copying is omitted. Stored or recently transferred FE sets the imported active glow across the network; it compiles but remains unverified in-world. Wrench reverse-cycle and other modes, color/facade behavior, and animated flow remain outstanding.
