# Remaining Arcforge scope

The full scope remains the [feature inventory](../../README.md#full-feature-inventory) and pinned upstream feature documentation. Items below are not complete just because their ids or recipe files exist. See [goal.md](../goal.md) for the completion bar and [handoff.md](../handoff.md) for current resume instructions.

## Progression and materials

- Match Halite's custom flat-bed geometry; the current vanilla feature preserves source frequency, size, and Y band as a cluster approximation. All standard Overworld ores, Halite, Nether Sulfur, both End Iolite placements, mountain/badlands Spodumene Pegmatite, and Heavy Mineral Sand now have placed features. Ten raw material types and eleven refined materials have pack/unpack storage recipes; Latex drying and sulfur vulcanizing have machine behavior and recipes in the Grain Dryer and Vulcanizer. The source Invar Dust craft now connects two Iron Dust and one Nickel Dust to the registered dust-to-crystal/ingot paths. Complete remaining storage blocks, crushing-to-dust paths, alloy obtainability, and source block properties. The registered ore/raw/dust recipe path now includes 32 smelting and 31 blasting recipes. Per-world Arcforge toggles remain fixed to source defaults because Horizon's content schema does not expose Arcforge's custom worldgen serializers/conditions.
- Complete remaining material progression and obtainability: Steel and all four source alloy/Graphite furnace recipes are implemented, and the Arcite/Tungsten Composite recipe is present; Arcforged Alloy remains blocked on Pitch/Carbon Fiber because its upstream Refinery source is not yet implemented. Add remaining machine bricks, tool materials, and supporting recipes.
- The core 13-block Treated Wood set, its nine crafting recipes, loot, and infusion inputs are present. The pinned source has no treated sign variants; add the remaining treated-wood-dependent farm/compost recipes. Pine Resin production, empty-hand collection, and Latex Bucket fluid exchange through the Resin Tap are present; its overflow uses Horizon's item-entity summon hook with a recovery fallback when spawning fails.

## Power, heat, and machines

- Finish Arcforge Furnace controls and its dedicated interface; its six-slot coke fuel/reagent handling, additive swapping, 3×3×6 formation check, heat progression, and 12-recipe steelmaking path are implemented. The Geothermal Plant's base lava and passive-contact heat generation is implemented; finish its upgrades, redstone/side controls, and dedicated interface. Finish Thermoelectric Plant controls/upgrades; its Wrench-configurable energy output faces are implemented. Extend Firebox Array with liquid/gas fuels, oxygen, flue gas, ports, upgrades, and its dedicated screen. Solar Thermal Array has its core structure, Wrench-configurable direct heat ports, and formed-block collar/heat-plate overlays; finish controls/status and dedicated screen, and verify the visuals and behavior in-world. Extend Combustion Plant controls, upgrades, and flue gas; its Wrench-configurable energy output faces are implemented.
- Energy conduits now pool FE across bidirectionally connected components and bridge one-way links between separate pools. Both FE and HU schedulers now use Horizon's separately supplied ticking entity; verify network rates, source/sink ordering, legacy charge migration, temperature behavior, and save/reload in-world. The static FE native capability still bypasses aggregate rate limits. Wrench reverse-cycle, animated energy flow, colors, and facades remain unfinished. The stored/recently moved active glow is implemented but still needs in-world visual verification. Four Item Conduit tiers now transfer directly between Wrench-configured Output and Input faces using Horizon's transactional item mover; verify item routing, limits, partial insertion, and save/reload in-world. Animated packets, conduit-held buffering, filters, and automatic endpoint-role detection remain unfinished. Fluid Conduit tiers now transfer one fluid type through configured/Auto-detected sided tanks at the lowest tier rate using Horizon's transactional mover; verify source selection, backpressure, rates, Auto faces, and save/reload in-world. Four Pressurized Conduit tiers and four Pressurized Cylinder/Gas Cartridge tiers recognize the same 22 pinned Arcforge gas IDs and have recipe/tag/loot data. Cylinders transfer gas with tiered cartridges through top/bottom inventory slots, retain gas across ordinary drops and placement, and expose a five-stage fill gauge. Rust and content builds pass; test registration, gas routing, rates, slot semantics, side behavior, drops/placement and save/reload in-world. Cylinder menus, comparator, gas tint, third-party gas classification, and content-preserving tier upgrades remain unfinished. Four Heat Cell tiers now store, leak, and transfer HU automatically and have recipes/loot; verify registration, flow limits, loss rates, charge drops, model state, and save/reload in-world. Their screen, capsule slots, insulation upgrades, side controls, redstone behavior, and charge-preserving tier upgrades remain unfinished. Lithium Cells and Battery Arrays remain unimplemented. Fluid conduit buffers, filters, and animated fill rendering remain unfinished. Energy Cell items preserve FE through ordinary player mining and placement; explosion loot uses a per-tick snapshot and needs in-world verification, while tier-upgrade recipes still lose charge. Their GUI, item charging slots, and orientation-aware sides remain unfinished. Fluid Tanks exchange partial fluid with Arcforge buckets and all four portable canisters through hand interactions; source automated canister slots/general item-container support, sided automation, and content-preserving tank/canister upgrades remain unfinished. Tier upgrades discard stored fluid because the pinned crafting-result hook does not expose ingredients.
- Finish processor variants and arrays: Crushing Array upgrades, ports and broader automation; Induction Furnace XP banking and arbitrary vanilla recipe lookup; Induction Array; Metal Pressing arrays; Chemical Reactor; Battery; and other machines in the source inventory.
- Complete Fiberizer/Infuser screens, output/input automation, upgrades, and upstream side behaviors.

- The basic Steel tools and armor are registered and craftable. Port the Steel Hammer and Excavator 3×3 area behavior when it can preserve block-entity protection, hardness checks, and per-block durability using supported hooks; add Foundry Suit, Long Fall Boots, Jetpacks, Arc Drill/Saw, modules, remaining Wrench modes, tooltips, HUDs, sounds, particles, custom machine screens, creative tabs, and manuals. The Wrench rotates supported single-block machines and configures Energy and Thermal Conduit, Solar heat-port, Combustion Plant, and Thermoelectric Plant faces; multiblocks, the Resin Tap, and other Wrench modes remain unsupported.
